**## The Short Version**

Burn does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All your session data stays on your device. The only external service the app uses is Google AdMob, which serves ads and collects standard advertising data as described below.

- --

**## 1. Information We Collect**

- ***We do not collect any personal information.****

Burn stores the following data ****locally on your device only****:

- Focus session history (duration, object type, activity tag, date)
- App settings (sound preference, selected activity)
- Earned badges and object unlock status

This data never leaves your device. We have no servers, no accounts, and no way to access your data.

- --

**## 2. Advertising — Google AdMob**

Burn shows ads provided by ****Google AdMob****. AdMob may collect and use the following information to serve relevant ads:

- Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android)
- Device type and operating system
- IP address (used for general location, not precise location)
- App usage data (time in app, features used)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

AdMob's data collection is governed by ****Google's Privacy Policy****:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

- ***You can opt out of personalised ads:****
- ***Android:**** Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation
- ***iOS:**** Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → Turn off Personalised Ads

Burn does ****not**** request or use:

- Precise location
- Camera or microphone
- Contacts or calendar
- Health data
